# SuperStore Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Short Description

An interactive Power BI analytics solution designed for **SuperStore Sales**, transforming raw transactional retail data into strategic business insights. The dashboard tracks core sales performance, analyzes profit margins, evaluates customer segments and shipping operational metrics, and incorporates predictive time-series modeling for 15-day sales forecasting.

## Tech Stack

* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop


* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query (ETL pipeline), Data Modeling


* **Analytical Expressions:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for dynamic KPIs, YoY time-intelligence calculations, and aggregations


* **Data Storage / Input:** Flat Files / CSV

## Data Source

* SuperStore Sales Dataset

## Key Features & Highlights

### 1. KPI Performance Overview

* **Sales Volume:** **1.57M** Total Sales generated.


* **Profitability:** **175.2K** Total Profit achieved.


* **Order Volume & Logistics:** **22K** Units sold with an **Average Delivery Time of 3.93 Days**.



### 2. Time-Series & Year-over-Year Analysis

* **Monthly Sales YoY:** Comparative trend analysis evaluating sales growth across 2019 and 2020.


* **Monthly Profit YoY:** Tracking seasonal profit fluctuations and year-over-year operational margins.



### 3. Regional & Segment Dissection

* **Geographic Distribution:** Interactive regional breakdown covering West (33%), East (29%), Central (22%), and South (16%) regions alongside map visualizations.


* **Customer Segment Breakdown:** Segmented by Consumer (48%), Corporate (33%), and Home Office (19%) buyer profiles.


* **Top Performing States:** Ranked breakdown led by California, New York, Texas, Washington, and Pennsylvania.



### 4. Category & Operational Insights

* **Product Category Performance:** Led by Office Supplies ($0.64M), Technology ($0.47M), and Furniture ($0.45M).


* **Top Sub-Categories:** High-performing product groups including Phones ($0.20M), Chairs ($0.18M), and Binders ($0.17M).


* **Fulfillment & Payment Preferences:** Payment distribution via Cash on Delivery (43%), Online (35%), and Card (22%), alongside Shipping Modes dominated by Standard Class ($0.33M).



### 5. Advanced Analytics & Predictive Forecasting

* **15-Day Sales Forecasting:** Machine-learning-based time-series forecasting projecting sales trajectory into January 2021 with confidence intervals.


* **Interactive Filtering:** Region slicers (Central, East, South, West) enabling global cross-filtering across all visuals.
